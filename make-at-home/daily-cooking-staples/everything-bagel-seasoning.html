Everything Bagel Seasoning

The most versatile two-minute seasoning blend in your kitchen. Goes on eggs, avocado toast, roasted vegetables, crackers, hummus, and — obviously — bagels. Once you make it once, you will put it on everything.

Prep2 mins
Cook0 mins
Makes~100g jar
Stores6 months
🥯
Ingredients
  • White sesame seeds2 tbsp
  • Black sesame seeds2 tbsp
  • Poppy seeds1 tbsp
  • Dried minced garlic1 tbsp
  • Dried minced onion1 tbsp
  • Flaky sea salt1 tsp
Storage
🫙
ContainerSmall airtight glass jar — sesame seeds oxidise quickly when exposed to air
📅
Shelf life6 months. The sesame seeds are the first to lose freshness — smell-test before using
💡
ProportionsThis ratio is balanced — adjust garlic and onion to your preference
🧂
Salt noteUse flaky sea salt, not fine table salt — the texture difference matters for this blend
Method

How to make it

  1. 1
    Measure all ingredients into a bowl

    No cooking, no toasting required — just combine. The ratio of seeds to aromatics to salt is what makes this blend work. Measure carefully for the first batch, then adjust to taste for subsequent ones.

  2. 2
    Mix thoroughly

    Stir well to ensure even distribution. The different sizes and densities of the ingredients mean they settle unevenly — thorough mixing at this stage means a more consistent blend from the jar.

  3. 3
    Transfer to a small glass jar

    Pour into a small glass jar with a tight-fitting lid. A jar with a wide mouth makes it easier to pinch and sprinkle. Label with the date.

  4. 4
    Optional — lightly toast before storing

    For a more intensely nutty flavour, toast the sesame and poppy seeds briefly in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until just fragrant before mixing. Cool completely before combining with the other ingredients.

The five-ingredient logic

The reason this blend is so universally good is that it hits five distinct flavour notes simultaneously: nuttiness (sesame), earthiness (poppy), pungency (garlic), sweetness (onion), and salinity (flaky salt). Each one is distinct enough to register separately but balanced enough that none overpowers. This is why it works on sweet things (avocado toast, yoghurt) as well as savoury ones — it is inherently balanced.

Use it in

Ways to use it

Pressed onto bagels before baking
On avocado toast
Sprinkled on fried or scrambled eggs
On roasted vegetables
On top of hummus
Mixed into cream cheese
Scattered on salads
On homemade crackers and flatbreads
Variations & tips
  • Add 1 tsp dried chilli flakes for a spicy version
  • Add 1 tsp dried rosemary for an Italian-inflected seasoning
  • Replace flaky salt with smoked salt for a deeper, more complex flavour
  • Add 1 tsp dried lemon zest for a bright, citrus-forward variation
  • Double the garlic and onion for a more pungent, deeply savoury version
