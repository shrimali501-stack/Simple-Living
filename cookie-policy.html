Cookie Policy

What cookies Sukara Living uses, why, and how to manage them.

Effective: January 2025 · Last updated: May 2025

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones Sukara Living uses, and how you can control them. By continuing to use sukaraliving.in, you consent to the use of cookies as described below.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work properly, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners about how their site is being used. Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses to your device.

Cookies we use

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are most read, how long people stay, and where they come from. This helps us create better content. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information anonymously. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Google Analytics cookies

_ga — distinguishes unique users. Expires after 2 years.
_ga_* — maintains session state. Expires after 2 years.
_gid — distinguishes users. Expires after 24 hours.

Essential cookies

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function. These include cookies set by our hosting provider (Netlify) for security and performance purposes. These cannot be disabled without affecting how the site works.

Font and third-party cookies

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set cookies or collect data when fonts are loaded. You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not use cookies for

Sukara Living does not use cookies for advertising, remarketing, behavioural profiling, or tracking you across other websites. We do not sell cookie data to any third party.

How to manage cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to do it in the most common browsers:

  • Chrome — Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari — Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the site.

Opt out of Google Analytics

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of cookies changes. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
